How to Play PuckLine

  • 1 The timeline orders active NHL players from Lowest Career Points (Left) to Highest Career Points (Right).
  • 2 Look at the active challenger card at the top, then tap any [+ Place Here] slot on the timeline (or drag & drop the card onto a slot).
  • 3 Ties are friendly! If a player has the exact same career points as an adjacent card, placing them on either side counts as correct.
  • 4 Live Feed Ready: Place a players.json file next to index.html in your GitHub repository to automatically load live post-match stats.
